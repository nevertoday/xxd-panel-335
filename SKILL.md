## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a zine or streetwear badge with handmade wear, not a centered corporate mark. Strong silhouette, thick line, flat shape, circles and arcs from one alphabet. It should stay recognizable at a small size, like a stamp on a package or a mark on a wall. Print may be rough. Avoid strict VI symmetry, fine detail, a polished mascot and a clean tech icon.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-335/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
